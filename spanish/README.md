# Spanish word lists

The most common Spanish words, ranked by frequency, with English meanings. Browse them on [apera.me/word-lists/spanish](https://apera.me/word-lists/spanish).

Columns: `rank`, `word` (the form as written; each form is its own row), `lemma` (dictionary form), `meaning` (English). The `.txt` files hold the words only, one per line, in rank order.

## How they were made

- **Speaking** is ranked using published film and TV subtitle word counts, most frequent first. Ranked from [FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords) by [Hermit Dave](https://github.com/hermitdave) ([CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)), which contains information from [OPUS OpenSubtitles](https://opus.nlpl.eu/) ([ODC-BY](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/)).
- **Reading: news** was counted by Apera from news pages on 245 sites, collected in 2021–2026.
- **Reading: sports** was counted by Apera from sports pages on 131 sites, collected in 2021–2026.
- **Reading: food** was counted by Apera from food pages on 107 sites, collected in 2021–2026.
- **Reading: tech** was counted by Apera from tech pages on 76 sites, collected in 2021–2026.

Full method and credits: [apera.me/sources](https://apera.me/sources).
